Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how AIT News Publisher ("the app", "we") accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data. The app is an internal tool that publishes content to the AIT YouTube channel, which we own and operate.

Data we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these YouTube Data API permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's videos.
  • youtube: to set video thumbnails, read and update the title and thumbnail of the channel's active live broadcast, and read the channel's own basic statistics.

The app does not ask for your email address, contacts, Gmail, Drive or any other Google data. It does not read comments, private messages, subscribers' details or any other user's account.

How we use the data

The data is used only to provide the features described on our home page: publishing videos to the AIT channel, keeping its live stream title and thumbnail current, and showing channel statistics on an internal status page. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, selling or training AI/ML models.

Storage and security

  • The OAuth token issued by Google is stored only on our own server, in a file with restricted access permissions. It is never placed in a public code repository or sent to any third party.
  • Upload history (video IDs, titles and upload times) is kept on the same server so the app can pace its uploads. No other YouTube data is stored.
  • All communication with Google uses HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google's own YouTube API to carry out the actions listed above.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept for as long as the app publishes to the channel. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that it can no longer act on the channel. To have the stored token and upload history deleted from our server, email us and we will delete them within 7 days.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AIT News Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
